# Third-party software, weights and data

A-nanTa does not embed pretrained model checkpoints, proprietary market datasets or third-party source snapshots. This list is informational, not a combined license grant.

**Open-source code projects:** AutoGluon, Chronos, Kronos, TimesFM, Time-MoE, EXAONE-Forecast, Toto, TiRex, NautilusTrader, HFTBacktest, Stable-Baselines3, MLflow, Optuna. Each retains its own license and attribution requirements.

**Weight-specific caution:** self-hosted TimesFM 3.0 pretrained weights and EXAONE Finance 1.0 pretrained weights have separate non-commercial terms by default. Operator-provided permissions may expand rights **only to their actual scope**. Confirm specific entity, demo/production rights, weight version, derivatives and effective dates. Keep private permission correspondence out of this public repo.

**Market-data caution:** exchange public archives, broker-exported ticks, HistData/TrueFX/FXCM and commercial source subscriptions can have terms restricting extraction, redistribution or commercial deployment even when access is free.

Before shipping an integration, record `upstream URL`, `version`, `weight hash`, `code license`, `weight license`, `data provenance` and `approval record ID`. A-nanTa's original source code currently has no declared distribution license.
